Breakthrough curve ​

This example simulates the direct column breakthrough (DCB) of Haghpanah et al. (2013), and how the heat released by adsorption shapes it. A two-component flue gas (CO₂ and N₂) is fed into a column of zeolite 13X initially filled with N₂, until CO₂ breaks through at the outlet. A breakthrough is a single step rather than a cycle, so it is run once with simulate.

julia
import Mocca
using Mocca.Units

Gas, adsorbent and column ​

The gas, adsorbent and column of the paper; the process from its parts example sets them up from their parameters.

julia
gas = Mocca.haghpanah_2013_gas()
feed = Mocca.Feed(gas; composition = (CO2 = 0.15, N2 = 0.85), temperature = 298.15, pressure = 1bar)
adsorbent = Mocca.load_adsorbent("zeolite_13x_haghpanah_2013")
column = Mocca.haghpanah_2013_column()
Column 1.0 m long, radius 0.1445 m (wall to 0.162 m), bed porosity 0.37

The gas velocity follows Darcy's law,

with the permeability k of a packed bed of particles of diameter and bed porosity from the Kozeny–Carman relation,

add_bed! computes it, and the axial dispersion, from the adsorbent and column. In the DCB of the paper no heat leaves the column, so the bed is adiabatic.

julia
ncells = 200
fs = Mocca.Flowsheet(gas, feed)
Mocca.add_bed!(fs, :Bed, adsorbent, column; ncells = ncells, thermal = Mocca.Adiabatic())
Mocca.add_device!(fs, :V_feed, :feed; at = (:Bed, :bottom))
Mocca.add_device!(fs, :V_product, :product; at = (:Bed, :top))

  Bed: fixed bed of zeolite 13X, 200 cells
    bottom V_feed (feed), from boundary at 1.0 bar
    top    V_product (product), to boundary at 1.0 bar

The breakthrough as a one-step cycle ​

A single adsorption step of 5000 s at an interstitial velocity of 1 m/s. The column starts at the feed pressure, the lowest pressure of this cycle, full of nitrogen.

julia
cycle = Mocca.Cycle([Mocca.Adsorb(5000.0; velocity = 1.0)])
process = Mocca.Process(fs, cycle; timestepping = Mocca.TimeStepping(max_dt = 5000.0, first_dt = 5000.0))

Each bed has a thermal model: Mocca.Adiabatic(), Mocca.WithWall() (the default), which adds an energy balance for the column wall and its heat exchange with the gas and the surroundings, or Mocca.Isothermal(T), with no energy balance. The same breakthrough with each:

julia
function breakthrough(thermal)
    fs = Mocca.Flowsheet(gas, feed)
    Mocca.add_bed!(fs, :Bed, adsorbent, column; ncells = ncells, thermal = thermal)
    Mocca.add_device!(fs, :V_feed, :feed; at = (:Bed, :bottom))
    Mocca.add_device!(fs, :V_product, :product; at = (:Bed, :top))
    p = Mocca.Process(fs, cycle; timestepping = process.timestepping)
    r = Mocca.simulate(p; timestep_selector_cfg = selector)
    return [s[:Bed] for s in r.states], r.timesteps
end
