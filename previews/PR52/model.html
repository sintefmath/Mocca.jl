Model equations ​

A fixed bed in Mocca is a one-dimensional, axially dispersed plug flow model of a packed column, as in Haghpanah et al. (2013), or, with radial_cells, one that also resolves the column's radius (see Beds meshed across their radius). The gas is ideal, flow follows Darcy's law, mass transfer to the sorbent is a linear driving force, and the energy balance covers the gas, the sorbent and the column wall. The beds are connected to the feed, products and each other through flow devices (valves and flow controllers), whose settings change from step to step of the cycle.

All quantities are in SI units. The symbols are listed in the nomenclature at the end of this page, with the inputs they come from.

Beds meshed across their radius ​

With radial_cells in add_bed!, the bed resolves the gradients across its radius as well as along it: the variables depend on and the radius , and the bed is symmetric about its axis. Discretisation ​

Each of the ncells cells along the column is split into radial_cells rings, uniform in radius unless radii gives their edges. A ring from to holds   ; the face between two rings at radius has the area  , and the face between two cells along the column the ring's cross-section. The fluxes are two-point, as along the column, over the distances between the centres of the rings.

  • The ports are the rings at each end. A stream into the bed is shared between them as their cross-sections are; a stream out of it takes each ring's own gas, and the device sees the rings mixed, at the mixing-cup temperature, so that every component and the enthalpy are conserved.

  • The wall exchanges heat with the outer ring only, through the film and the half of the ring between its centre and the wall,    for the ring's centre (RingWallResistance). Without the half ring the error would be first order in the width of the rings; with it, the cooling of a bed by conduction alone matches the series solution to second order (see Conduction across the radius).

  • The cells inside the outer ring have no wall: their wall temperature follows their gas temperature, and has no meaning.

A bed of one radial cell is the 1D bed of the sections above.

Numerical method ​

Each bed is divided into ncells equal cells (30 by default), and each of those into radial_cells rings for a bed meshed across its radius. Fluxes between cells are two-point, with upwinded advection, and time stepping is fully implicit (backward Euler), solved by Newton's method with exact derivatives from automatic differentiation. Each step of the cycle starts with time steps of first_dt that double up to max_dt (see TimeStepping), which carries the solver through the abrupt start of a step. Gradients for optimisation come from the adjoint of the same discrete equations.
