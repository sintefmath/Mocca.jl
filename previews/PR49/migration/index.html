Migrating from Mocca 0.1.0

Mocca 0.1.0 described a process by a constants struct, boundary conditions and stage times. These were removed in favour of the objects in the Reference:

RemovedUse instead
adsorptionConstants with FixedBed(constants), setup_process_model(system, constants), DualSiteLangmuir(constants)Gas, Adsorbent, Column and add_bed!
setup_boundary_conditions, setup_forces(model, stage_times, bcs) and the AdsorptionBC, PressurisationBC, BlowdownBC, EvacuationBC boundary conditionsa flowsheet with devices and a Cycle, run as a Process
setup_mocca_caseread_process and Process files
four_stage_vsa_flowsheet, two_bed_vsa_flowsheet, lpp_vsa_flowsheet, dual_adsorbent_vsa_flowsheetfour_stage_vsa_process, two_bed_vsa_process, lpp_vsa_process, dual_adsorbent_vsa_process
wet_flue_gas_isotherm, wet_flue_gas_constants, wet_flue_gas_system, wet_flue_gas_initial_stateload_adsorbent, krishnamurthy_2014_gas, krishnamurthy_2014_column
Column() for the unit entityUnit()

The constants structs remain for moving inputs over: HaghpanahConstants, or Mocca.parse_input for a JSON file of Mocca 0.1.0, gives a struct that convert_constants turns into the gas, feed, adsorbent and column of a Flowsheet.

Mocca.convert_constants — Function
convert_constants(constants) → NamedTuple

The Gas, Feed, Adsorbent and Column described by a HaghpanahConstants or adsorptionConstants struct, and its operating conditions: p_high, p_intermediate and p_low [Pa], the superficial feed velocity v_feed and the interstitial velocity [m/s], the ramp rate λ [1/s] and the dispersion_velocity [m/s] the struct used.
